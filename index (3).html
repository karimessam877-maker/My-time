    When you can’t start

    1. Promise yourself only 2 minutes. Starting is the hard part.
    2. Put your phone in another room and close every tab except the one you need.
    3. Write the next physical action, like “open the file and write the first heading.”
    4. After a job shift, take a 20 minute reset first (water, food, walk), then do one 25 minute round.
    5. End every day by starring tomorrow’s 3 priorities.
